# 240 English keyword candidates for Invitra

Audience: worldwide English searches. Prepared 2026-10-27.

This bank contains 240 unique phrases in 16 intent clusters.
The phrases are research candidates inferred from the product, prior search-result
checks and topic expansion. They are not 240 independently verified queries or
measured high-volume/low-competition terms. Volume, difficulty and rankings remain
unverified. P1/P2 indicate product relevance and publishing order, not SEO scores.

Use one useful page for a coherent intent cluster, with natural supporting language.
Do not paste all phrases into pages, meta-keywords or hidden text, or generate one
page per phrase. A mapped URL is the nearest existing coverage, not proof that
every topic in the cluster has been answered. Catalog query filters canonicalize
to /templates and are not standalone search landing pages.

[Download the spreadsheet-ready CSV](keyword-research.csv).
See [the growth plan](../SEARCH-GROWTH.md) for the current research sources and
Search Console measurement steps.
